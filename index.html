Field notes

What we end up writing
to almost everyone.

Short answers to the things that come up in every brief. Take them whether or not you travel with us.

Packing

Three climates, one 40-litre bag

  • Merino layers beat a puffer for any trip crossing 20°C of range.
  • Two pairs of shoes, both already broken in — one of them waterproof.
  • A flat packable day bag, so the big one stays at the hotel.
  • Plan to wash every fifth day; it beats carrying a heavier bag.

Tables

Getting a table where there is no website

  • Ask three weeks ahead, not three days — and ask for lunch.
  • Lunch is easier to secure at the places worth the journey.
  • Give the restaurant your hotel as the backup contact number.
  • Say yes to the 6pm seating; kitchens are calmer and the bread is warm.

With children

Long-haul with a small person

  • Fly overnight in their direction of travel, not yours.
  • One new thing per four hours of flight, revealed slowly.
  • Book the row behind the bulkhead, never the bulkhead itself.
  • Day one is a slow day — build it into the route, not around it.
